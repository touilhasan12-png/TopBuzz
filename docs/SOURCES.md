# Research sources and attribution ledger

These are the initial fact-check references. The final narration and end credits must be checked against the sources directly before final render.

1. Encyclopaedia Britannica, “Istanbul” — city history, naming, capital move, and Bosporus bridges: https://www.britannica.com/place/Istanbul
2. Encyclopaedia Britannica, “Istanbul: Constantinople” — Constantine's New Rome, 1204, 1261, and 1453 context: https://www.britannica.com/place/Istanbul/Constantinople
3. Encyclopaedia Britannica, “Fall of Constantinople (1453)” — date and siege context: https://www.britannica.com/event/Fall-of-Constantinople-1453
4. UNESCO World Heritage Centre, “Historic Areas of Istanbul” — heritage description and principal monuments: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/356/
5. UNESCO property documentation — Theodosian land-wall context and major Byzantine/Ottoman architectural works: https://whc.unesco.org/document/216600
6. Encyclopaedia Britannica, “Bosporus” — strait and bridge context: https://www.britannica.com/place/Bosporus
7. Istanbul Tarihi, chronology: https://istanbultarihi.ist/chronology

## Data / asset credits
- If OpenStreetMap data is used, include “© OpenStreetMap contributors” and comply with the ODbL attribution/share-alike obligations: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Record each third-party model, texture, font, and sound asset in `ASSET_CREDITS.csv` with its author, source URL, license, and modifications.
- Prefer original procedural geometry for period city blocks. Do not download/reuse film footage or copyrighted music without permission.
